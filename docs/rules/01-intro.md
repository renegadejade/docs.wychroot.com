# Welcome to the Underground

_Wychroot: No Gods, No Masters_ is a political science-fantasy tabletop RPG about coming… . We play an affinity group, or _crew_ of unlikely militants who have come together to fight a growing existential threat.
The Dominion of Mars has returned to Earth 300 years after climate apocalypse, not expecting to find human societies left on the forsaken planet. However, they found us, the leftovers, the descendants of those left to die when those with means fled to the red plant.
Claiming authority over us at once, the Dominion mistook us as an easy target. But in the time since they left, we learned no only to survive, but to thrive. And we’re fighting back.

## Solarpunk in the Dark

_Wychroot_ is a _Forged in the Dark_ game, which means its core rules are based on the rules developed for _Blades in the Dark_ by John Harper and published by Evil Hat. While _Blades_ is about scoundrels building a profitable crime syndicate, Wychroot takes Harper’s system to a different setting—a post-apocalypse Earth, where small communities live under solarpunk ideas.
Solarpunk is an art movement, a literature genre, an aesthetic, and an imagination of what life could be in a post-scarcity society where nature, technology, and humanity can live in symbiosis together. It rejects the neon-noir dystopia of its predecessor, cyberpunk, in favor a future without capitalism, inequity, or tyranny. While those with solarpunk ideals hope to avoid climate apocalypse, in Wychroot, it has already happened. But in its wake, the leftovers on Earth have found themselves living in communities with solarpunk ideals.

## Collaborative Storytelling

At its core, Wychroot is a game of collaborative storytelling between everyone at the table, both players and GM. **We play to find out what happens**, working together to weave a unique and engaging narrative. While the GM drives some of the broader direction of the game, the players are just as important in building the shared world.
